VeloTimer is a countdown and stopwatch app designed for track cyclists and their coaches.

It is built as a website which can be installed on your phone for offline use. Look for an `Install` or `Add To Home Screen` option in your browser's menu.

# Countdown seconds

When you press `Start`, a countdown will begin. Set the desired duration of the countdown on the Settings tab. The app's stopwatch will begin automatically when the countdown reaches zero.

# Audio pattern

VeloTimer replicates the _beep beep beeeeep!_ of a velodrome's countdown clock. In addition to the standard pattern, we can replicate the tones and durations of other velodromes. For extra authenticity, you can even add an echo!

# Pursuit mode

Enter the planned times for your first and other laps. Each time you press `Lap`, the device will briefly show the difference against your planned race schedule, on a red or green background.

# Save and Compare

When a ride ends, you see an option to `Save` the ride to a database within your app or browser. You can find times based on rider and/or distance, and choose two rides to `Compare` their splits side-by-side. You can also `Export` your database in CSV format, suitable for spreadsheets and other data analysis software.
